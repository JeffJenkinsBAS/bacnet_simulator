Tuning practice

Opening VAV dampers lowers static pressure and calls for more fan speed. Closing dampers produces a short pressure rise before the PID unloads the VFD. The PID signal scales 0–100% to 0–60 Hz; while commanded on, the drive clamps its physical output to a 20 Hz minimum (33.3% speed). Increase P for a stronger immediate response, add I to remove steady-state error, and introduce D cautiously.
